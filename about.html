One Person, Not a Publisher

No agency, no freelance content pool, no paid rankings. This is a personal site about a country I have spent years trying to understand.

Why this exists

I built the reference I wished I had

I am an independent writer who has spent years reading about China, cooking its food, and asking people who live there what the books leave out. The guidebooks I bought listed the same twenty sights in the same order and explained almost none of them. What I wanted was smaller and harder: why the wall turns where it turns, why one province's noodles taste nothing like the next, and what a temple layout is actually saying.

Learn About China is my attempt to write that down properly — travel, culture, history and food, explained for people who did not grow up here and do not read Chinese. It is not a booking platform and it is not owned by any travel company.

How pages get made

The boring process behind each page

This site is not built out of trips I have taken. It is built out of sources I can name: UNESCO nomination files, provincial heritage office publications, museum catalogues, Chinese-language local histories and government statistics. Every figure on a page traces back to one of them, and every page shows the month it was last checked.

Culture and history pages are written from museum labels, academic overviews and Chinese-language sources rather than from other English travel blogs, which is why the dynasty maps are redrawn from geographic data instead of copied from a poster. Where sources disagree — and they often do — I say so in the text.

If a page is thin, it gets cut rather than padded. I would rather publish four paragraphs I can defend than forty I cannot.

What you will not find here

No paid placements, no medical advice

Nobody pays to be listed, ranked or removed. Display ads cover hosting; they are chosen by Google, not by me, and they have no influence on what gets written. There are no affiliate links in the recommendations.

Health content was taken offline deliberately. Hospitals and regional specialties are a real topic, but they belong in a clinical context, not in a travel guide written by one person. Nothing on this site is medical advice.

Where I have stood in a place, the page says so. Where I have not, it is built from named sources rather than from other English blogs, and the sources are listed at the bottom of the page. If something is wrong or out of date, tell me and I will fix it and note the correction on the page.

Corrections & contact

Tell me what is wrong

One pair of eyes misses things. If a date is wrong, a name is misspelled, a translation is off, or a historical claim does not hold up, write to me with the page and what you saw. Corrections are made in the page itself rather than quietly deleted.
