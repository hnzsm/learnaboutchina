Privacy Policy

What this site collects, what it does not, and how to control the cookies used to serve advertising.

Last updated 16 November 2026

This site is a reference work about China. It has no accounts, no comments and no newsletter, so most of the data questions other sites have to answer do not arise here. What does arise is advertising, because the site is funded by Google AdSense, and advertising uses cookies.

Who runs this site

LearnAboutChina is written and maintained by one independent publisher. There is no company behind it, no agency and no advertising sales team of its own. Questions about this policy, or about anything published here, can go to learnaboutchinaorg@gmail.com.

What this site does not collect

There are no user accounts, no comments, no contact forms, no newsletter sign-up and no file uploads. Nothing on the site asks for your name, address, phone number or payment details. The only personal data you can send is an email, and only if you choose to write.

Cookies and advertising

Pages carry Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and to other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits.

You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Google describes its own practices in its advertising technologies pages.

Where the law requires consent before advertising cookies are set, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google may show a consent message first. Declining does not block the site; it only means ads are less relevant to you.

What this site stores in your browser

If you switch the site into Chinese, that choice is kept in your browser's local storage under the key lac-lang, so the next page you open stays in the language you picked. It contains a language code and nothing else. Clearing site data in your browser removes it, and the site falls back to English.

Server logs

The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes standard request data such as IP address, browser type, requested URL and time of the request, in order to deliver pages and block abuse. These logs are not combined with advertising data, and this site does not build profiles from them.

No analytics

There is no Google Analytics, no third-party analytics script and no tracking pixel on any page. Page counts are not measured per visitor here.

External links

Articles cite sources such as UNESCO, museum publications and academic papers, and those links leave this site. Once you follow one, the destination's own privacy policy applies. No third-party content is embedded in these pages, and all images are served from this domain.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and no data is knowingly collected from them.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site still works without them, apart from remembering your language. You can also use the two advertising opt-outs linked above. If a consent message has been shown to you, the same message lets you change your answer later.

Changes to this policy

If what the site does changes, this page changes with it, and the date at the top is updated. Substantial changes will be described here rather than made silently.

Contact

Write to learnaboutchinaorg@gmail.com. Questions about the content of an article, including corrections, are answered the same way.
